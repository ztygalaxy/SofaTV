# Channel logo assets

The PNG files in this directory were selected from the
[`fanmingming/live`](https://github.com/fanmingming/live) television icon library.
They are bundled locally and are never fetched while the app is running.

The source library is licensed under GNU GPL v3; a copy is included as
`LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`. Broadcaster names and marks remain the property of their
respective owners and are used only to identify channels.
